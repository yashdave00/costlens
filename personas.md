## Persona Insights for CTR Optimization

### Top 10 converting personas (highest willingness to pay):
1. Solo founders with $1K+ MRR and growing cloud bills (Sarah, David, Liam)
2. Startup founders post-fundraise with board pressure (Alex, Jordan, Taylor)
3. Non-technical founders who vibe-coded their app and don't understand costs (Alex non-tech, Jordan non-tech)
4. DevOps engineers inheriting messy infra (Riley Garcia, Drew Rodriguez)
5. Agency owners looking to white-label audits (Casey Zhang, Sage Robinson)

### CTR optimization recommendations:
1. **Hero headline**: "Find the cloud money your codebase is burning." → Keep, it's punchy and specific
2. **CTA**: "Get the free scanner" → Add "in 5 seconds" for urgency
3. **Social proof**: Add "Used by X founders" or "Saved $X in cloud costs"
4. **Pricing**: Lead with $29 Scout, not $2000 Architect (anchor high, sell low)
5. **Trust signals**: Add "No credit card required" near CTAs
6. **Urgency**: Add "Limited to 10 audits/week" for Probe+ tiers
7. **Risk reversal**: "Full refund if no savings found" (already in Architect tier)
8. **Mobile**: Ensure CTA is sticky on mobile
9. **Speed**: Page should load in <2s (currently heavy with animations)
10. **Personalization**: Show different hero text for different referrers (HN vs Twitter vs Reddit)
